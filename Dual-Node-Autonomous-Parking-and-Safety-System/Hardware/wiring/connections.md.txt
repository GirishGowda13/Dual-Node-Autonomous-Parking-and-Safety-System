# Hardware Wiring

## Node 1 ↔ Node 2 UART

 |Node 1  | Node 2  |
 |--------|---------|
 |RC6 (TX)| RC7 (RX)|
 |RC7 (RX)| RC6 (TX)|
 |GND     | GND     |

## Node 1

- Ultrasonic sensors connected according to `node1pinout.md`
- LCD connected to PORTD
- LEDs connected to RC0–RC3
- Buzzer connected to RC4
- Servo connected to RC5

## Node 2

- LDR connected to RA0AN0
- Ultrasonic sensors connected to RB0–RB3
- Rain gauge connected to RB4
- Status LEDs connected to RC0–RC2
- Buzzer connected to RC5
- LCD connected to PORTD
